# NuralCampus: The Intelligent Academic Ecosystem[cite: 1]

Live Demo: https://nuralcampus.vercel.app/[cite: 1]

NuralCampus is an intelligent, community-driven academic resource management platform designed to centralize and streamline access to educational materials for university students and educators.[cite: 1] It acts as a cohesive digital ecosystem that connects students, resources, alumni, and administration.[cite: 1]

## Problem Statement & Objective

Students frequently struggle with fragmented study materials scattered across personal drives, WhatsApp groups, and emails.[cite: 1] Furthermore, there is no structured mechanism to recognize and reward peer contributors who share high-quality materials.[cite: 1]

NuralCampus solves this by providing a centralized, searchable E-Library organized by department, year, semester, and course.[cite: 1] It gamifies knowledge sharing through a contributor leaderboard and facilitates collaborative learning with per-resource reactions and nested comments.[cite: 1]

## Key Features

* Centralized E-Library: Search and filter academic resources (Books, Notes, Slides) by Department, Year, Semester, and Course.[cite: 1]
* Contributor Leaderboard: A gamified ranking system that dynamically updates based on a user's total upload count, rewarding active community members with rank badges (Gold, Silver, Bronze).[cite: 1]
* Secure Authentication: JWT-based session management ensuring secure login, registration, and protected routes.[cite: 1]
* Discussion & Feedback: Per-resource comment sections supporting nested replies and reactions.[cite: 1]
* Site-Wide Reviews: A dedicated system for users to submit 1-5 star ratings and share their experience with the platform.[cite: 1]
* Comprehensive User Profiles: Showcases a user's contributor stats, total uploads, earned achievements, and a quick-action upload panel.[cite: 1]
